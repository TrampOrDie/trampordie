About Tramp Or Die

Tramp Or Die isn’t a brand idea — it’s a reality.

It comes from the world of outside construction, where you either adapt, travel, learn, and grind… or you get left behind. This site was built for people who choose to move forward, even when it’s uncomfortable.

What “Tramp Or Die” Means

To tramp isn’t about bouncing around for fun. It’s about doing what needs to be done to provide, to grow, and to stay relevant in a trade that doesn’t wait on anyone.

Sometimes that means:

  • Taking calls in unfamiliar places
  • Working long hours, bad weather, and tough conditions
  • Leaving comfort behind to gain experience
  • Betting on yourself when nothing is guaranteed

Tramp Or Die is the mindset that says:
I’m not stuck. I’m not done. I’ll figure it out.

Why This Exists

There’s a lot of gatekeeping, half-answers, and outdated advice in the trades. And there’s almost no place that breaks things down clearly for someone trying to:

  • Transition into outside construction
  • Travel for work
  • Understand books, calls, locals, and expectations
  • Prepare mentally and practically for the lifestyle

This site exists to change that.

No hype.
No bullshit.
Just clear, practical guidance built from real experience.

Our Mission

The mission of Tramp Or Die is simple: to give working tradesmen clarity, preparation, and confidence in a system that’s often confusing, gatekept, or poorly explained.

This site exists to:

  • Break complex processes into clear, usable steps
  • Remove unnecessary guesswork from career and travel decisions
  • Preserve hard-earned knowledge that’s often passed down inconsistently — or not at all
  • Help people make informed moves, not desperate ones

We believe no one should be set up to fail just because they didn’t have the right mentor, the right timing, or the right information.

How We Do It

  • Practical guidance — not theory
  • Real-world expectations — not ideal scenarios
  • Preparation over panic
  • Ownership over excuses

Everything here is designed to be used — on the road, in the yard, or in the moment you need to make a decision fast.

Who This Is For

This is for:

  • Apprentices looking ahead
  • Journeymen thinking about their next move
  • Anyone tired of guessing and ready to take ownership of their path

If you’re looking for shortcuts or hand-holding, this probably isn’t it.

If you’re looking for clarity, preparation, and a mindset that survives the long haul — you’re in the right place.

The Bottom Line

You don’t need permission to improve your situation.
You don’t need perfect timing.
You just need direction, discipline, and the willingness to move.

Tramp Or Die is about choosing movement over stagnation — every time.
